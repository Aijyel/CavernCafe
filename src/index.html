Our Story

Nestled in the heart of the village, The Cozy Chalice was forged from stone and dreams in the year of our Lord 1325. Our mission? To serve hearty brews and warm meals in a space that feels like home—torch-lit walls, carved wood, and tales aplenty.

Whether you're a weary traveler seeking comfort, a local seeking community, or a bard looking for an audience, our doors are open to all who respect the ways of the realm and bring good cheer to our hall.

Meet the Innkeeper

Lady Evelyn of Brewshire

Master of elixirs and keeper of warmth since the founding of our establishment. Born to a family of herbalists and brewers, Lady Evelyn brings generations of knowledge to each cup and plate served at The Cozy Chalice.

With a smile as warm as our hearth and wisdom passed down through ages, she welcomes weary travelers and dreamers alike into the comfort of our humble establishment.
