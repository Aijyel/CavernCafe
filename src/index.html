Our Tale

Nestled betwixt the ancient oaks and standing stones of Evermere, The Cozy Chalice has served travelers since the year of our Lord 1325. Built of stone hewn from the nearby quarry and timber felled from the enchanted forest, our establishment offers sanctuary to all manner of folk.

Whether ye be knight errant, traveling merchant, or simple wanderer seeking respite from the harsh world beyond, our hearth burns bright to welcome you. Here tales are spun, friendships forged, and weary souls renewed.

The Keeper of the Chalice

Miss Jia of Abraham

Born to a family of renowned herbalists and brewers, Jia inherited ancient recipes and wisdom passed through generations. Her hands, weathered by decades of crafting elixirs and hearty fare, work with the practiced precision of a master artisan.

In her youth, she traveled the known world learning the brewing secrets of distant lands before returning to establish The Cozy Chalice. Now she offers her knowledge and warmth to all who enter, treating commoner and nobility alike with equal measure of respect and care.
